# Data Retention

This document explains how long DocMind keeps customer data, how backups are
handled, and how a customer can request deletion.

## Active accounts

Documents, chunks, embeddings, and chat logs are retained for as long as the
account is active. Chat transcripts are kept for 180 days by default; customers
on the Enterprise plan can reduce this to 30 days in the tenant settings.

## After cancellation

When a subscription is cancelled, customer data is retained for 90 days to
allow reactivation. After 90 days the tenant is deleted, including documents,
embeddings, and chat logs. Deletion is irreversible once the 90-day window
closes.

## Backups

Encrypted backups are taken every six hours and are retained for 35 days.
Backups are stored in a separate region and are used only for disaster
recovery. Restoring a backup does not restore data that was deleted before the
backup was taken.

## Deletion requests

Customers can request deletion of personal data at any time. Verified deletion
requests are completed within 30 days, and a deletion certificate is issued to
the customer. Legal holds and billing records take precedence over deletion
requests where the law requires retention.

## Subprocessors

A current list of subprocessors is published on the trust page. Customers are
notified 30 days before a new subprocessor receives access to customer data.
