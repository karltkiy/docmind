# Refund Policy

DocMind sells software subscriptions and, in limited cases, preconfigured
appliance hardware. This policy explains when a customer can request a refund,
how the request is reviewed, and how long the money takes to come back.

## Refund window

A new subscription can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. The
window starts on the date the first invoice is paid, not on the date the
account is created. Refund requests are accepted through the billing portal or
by email to the billing team. A request is considered received once the
customer receives an automatic confirmation number.

## Opened hardware

Hardware that has been unboxed and connected to a network carries a 15%
restocking fee. Unopened hardware is refunded in full when it is returned in
its original packaging. The restocking fee is calculated from the list price,
not from the discounted price paid by the customer.

## Review and processing

Every request is reviewed by the billing team within three business days. Once
approved, the refund is issued within ten business days to the original payment
method. Bank transfers can take an additional two business days to appear on
the customer statement. Refunds are never issued as account credit unless the
customer explicitly asks for credit.

## Exceptions

Annual plans are refundable only during the first 30 days. After that period,
unused months cannot be refunded but can be applied to a future renewal.
Professional services, training hours, and custom onboarding work are not
refundable once they have been delivered.

## Chargebacks

Customers are asked to contact billing before opening a chargeback. A chargeback
that is later withdrawn still triggers a 25 USD administrative fee.
