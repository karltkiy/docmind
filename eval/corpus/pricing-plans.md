# Pricing Plans

DocMind is sold as a monthly or annual subscription. Prices are listed in US
dollars and exclude local taxes. Every plan includes document upload, vector
search, and grounded chat completions over the customer corpus.

## Starter

The Starter plan costs 49 USD per month and includes up to 5 seats, 1,000
uploaded documents, and 20,000 chat requests per month. Storage is limited to
10 GB. The plan is designed for small teams that are evaluating retrieval
quality on their own documents.

## Team

The Team plan costs 199 USD per month and includes up to 25 seats. It raises
the chat request quota to 250,000 per month and adds role-based access control,
audit logs, and priority support. Additional seats cost 9 USD each per month.

## Enterprise

Enterprise pricing is quoted per contract and starts at 1,500 USD per month.
The Enterprise plan adds a dedicated cluster, custom data retention, single
sign-on, and a 99.99% uptime commitment. A 12-month minimum term applies to
every Enterprise contract.

## Overage and discounts

Requests above the plan quota are billed at 0.02 USD per API call, with a hard
cap of 35% of the base monthly fee. Annual billing receives a 20% discount
compared with twelve monthly payments. Non-profit organizations can request an
additional 10% discount through their account manager.

## Billing rules

Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing cycle. Payment is due
within 15 days. Accounts more than 30 days past due are suspended after a
written notice, and data is retained according to the retention policy.
