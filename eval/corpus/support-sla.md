# Support SLA

This document describes the support service levels for DocMind cloud customers
on the Team and Enterprise plans. Starter customers receive the same severity
definitions but best-effort handling.

## Severity levels

P1 covers a production outage or data loss. P2 covers a major feature that is
unavailable with no workaround. P3 covers degraded performance or a single user
that is blocked. P4 covers questions, documentation requests, and minor
cosmetic issues.

## Response targets

The first response target for P1 is 15 minutes, measured from the moment the
ticket is created. P2 has a first response target of one hour. P3 has a first
response target of four business hours, and P4 has a first response target of
two business days. P1 tickets are handled 24/7 by the on-call rotation; P2 and
P3 tickets are handled during business hours from 09:00 to 18:00 CET.

## Uptime commitment

The monthly uptime commitment for the Team plan is 99.95%. Enterprise customers
can negotiate a 99.99% commitment with a dedicated cluster. Uptime is measured
on the chat completion endpoint and excludes maintenance announced at least 48
hours in advance.

## Service credits

When monthly uptime falls below 99.95%, the customer receives a 10% monthly
service credit. When uptime falls below 99.5%, the credit rises to 25% of the
monthly fee. Credits are applied to the next invoice and are capped at one
month of fees. Credits are the only remedy offered for missed uptime targets.

## Escalation

A P1 ticket that is not acknowledged within 15 minutes is automatically
escalated to the support manager. Customers on the Enterprise plan also get a
named technical account manager and a shared escalation channel.
